What is available today

Official-source court search: guided access to Ohio, Nevada and Florida public court portals, with no purchase or account required for the finder. Individual results depend on the official court websites.

Record relief: self-service eligibility information and court-packet workflows. Verify jurisdiction-specific rules and official court forms before filing.

Correction and mugshot removal: free request guidance for third-party sites and search engines.

Second-chance entrepreneurship: business tools, mentorship and funding resources.

What's still in development

RecordPathAI does not currently operate a nationwide criminal-record database. It does not automatically import every court's records or guarantee expungement, case removal, court acceptance or e-filing. Some paid packet and monitoring features require additional production verification before they can be promoted as generally available.

Actual personal case data will not be added to our own public search solely from historical bulk files. Publication requires verified rights, current status and a correction or suppression process.

Free correction requests and privacy

There is no charge to submit a correction or removal request concerning information published by RecordPathAI. The guided court-source finder does not require a payment. Please read our privacy policy before using account or paid features.

RecordPathAI provides informational software, not legal advice, legal representation or a guarantee of any legal outcome. Availability varies by court and jurisdiction. This platform must not be used to make FCRA-regulated employment, housing, credit or insurance eligibility decisions.
