What's still in development

RecordPathAI does not operate a nationwide criminal-record database, automatically import every court's records, or guarantee expungement, case removal, court acceptance or e-filing. The final signed-packet generation/payment, court-fee collection, Lob print-and-mail integration, and tracking dashboard are still being connected. The $189 combined filing service is not currently a live purchase.

Actual personal case data will not be added to our own public search solely from historical bulk files. Publication requires verified rights, current status and a correction or suppression process.
