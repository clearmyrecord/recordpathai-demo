Court & Clerk Dashboard

Court Access is a partner-facing workspace for onboarded court personnel, clerk teams, agency administrators, and authorized justice-system reviewers. The dashboard is designed to help court-side users review completed user packets, verify required fields, inspect supporting documents, flag missing information, and manage submission status using role-based access and audit trails.

Online Filing Intake

RecordPathAI is designed to route completed packets from eligible users to participating clerks or court partners for clerk review, acceptance, correction, or filing preparation. Intake workflows can support structured packet submission, secure packet transmission, jurisdiction-specific packet routing, filing status updates, and court-approved submission rules for participating jurisdictions.

Structured packets

Packet data is organized into consistent fields for review, validation, document inspection, and downstream filing preparation.

Participating courts

Routing can be configured around participating courts, onboarded clerks, agency partners, and jurisdiction-specific submission rules.

Secure transmission

The platform architecture supports secure packet transfer patterns, correction requests, notification workflows, and status updates.

Court Onboarding
