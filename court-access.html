How Clerk Access Works

Court Access is designed for the people and partner organizations that receive, review, and process court-ready record-relief filings.

1

Applicant generates court-ready packet.

2

Packet is submitted digitally or by print/mail.

3

Clerk receives structured packet data.

4

Clerk reviews filing requirements.

5

Clerk marks status: received, incomplete, accepted, filed, hearing scheduled, granted, denied.

6

Applicant receives status updates.

Court Partner Data Model

The Court Access foundation is organized around future Supabase-backed entities that can support court staff permissions, packet intake, filing status history, document requests, and auditability.

court_users

Authorized clerks, administrators, filing reviewers, partner viewers, and super admins.

courts

Court, county, jurisdiction, filing URL, and digital packet acceptance metadata.

court_staff_roles

Role definitions and permission scopes for court-specific portal access.

packet_submissions

Structured packet records linked to applicants, courts, case IDs, PDFs, and status.

filing_status_events

Append-only status history for received, incomplete, filed, hearing, and outcome changes.

court_notes

Internal and applicant-visible notes tied to each packet submission.

document_requests

Correction and missing-document requests that can be surfaced to applicants.

audit_logs

Security and compliance trail for every court-side action.

Access Roles

Clerk

Reviews incoming packets, updates filing statuses, and requests corrections.

Court Administrator

Manages court staff access and oversees court-specific filing operations.

Filing Reviewer

Reviews filing requirements and prepares packets for formal processing.

Read-only Partner

Views assigned packet and status information without changing records.

Super Admin

Manages courts, role policies, templates, and integration readiness across jurisdictions.
