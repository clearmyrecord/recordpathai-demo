One filing, not duplicate fees

When court rules permit several charges or cases on a single sealing application, RecordPathAI's intended pricing is one RecordWatch fee and one packet preparation fee for that application, plus the applicable court charge. Cases needing different applications are priced separately.

Current checkout limitation: The live $39 RecordWatch checkout is still linked to one saved case. The grouped-application entitlement and checkout are not yet enabled. Before buying multiple upgrades for cases that may share a single application, contact RecordPathAI to avoid duplicate purchases.
