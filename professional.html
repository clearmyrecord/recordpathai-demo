01 / INTAKE

Take on more matters with confidence

02 / REVIEW

Focus on legal work, not data entry

Review eligibility estimates, waiting periods and risk flags using a consistent workflow. You retain professional judgment and review the full case before filing.

03 / DOCUMENTS

Stop rebuilding the same paperwork

Prepare supported court-specific conviction and nonconviction applications from saved client and case details. Document preparation is included in your subscription.

04 / RECORDWATCH

Find every saved application

Generate and save drafts and official unsigned forms against the correct client and case. Reopen archived packets and keep signed document reviews together.

05 / FOLLOW-UP

Keep your cases moving

Use case reminders, hearing and filing deadline tracking, and independently verified court events to organize follow-up. Live docket polling and email/SMS alerts are not yet available.

06 / CONTROL

Private case access, two-step verification and explicit document review help keep client files organized. Obtain the actual applicant signature and handle court submission through the supported manual filing process.
