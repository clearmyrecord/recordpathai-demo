Purpose-built professional workflow

A dedicated suite for your practice

Designed to help legal teams reduce repetitive administrative work while retaining professional judgment.

01 / INTAKE

Client & case management

Separate client and court details. Designed around assigned matters, organization permissions and multifactor access.

02 / REVIEW

Attorney-led eligibility

Counsel reviews case facts, applicable law and the complete docket. Uncertain or mixed dispositions are held for review.

03 / DOCUMENTS

Court-specific preparation

Wood County conviction and nonconviction forms, with a separate exact-document approval step.

04 / SIGNING

Attorney signing

Staging workflow uses verified counsel credentials and explicit consent. Court acceptance of attorney-only signatures is pending.

05 / FILING

Optional filing assistance

Planned court-approved automated filing. Attorneys retain submission authority; no automated submissions are enabled.

06 / MONITORING

RecordWatch Professional

Production case reminders and independently verified court-event tracking. Live docket polling and email/SMS alerts remain unavailable.

Initial market: Wood County Court of Common Pleas. Expansion prioritizes courts without required notarization or Social Security numbers and with compatible intake fields.
