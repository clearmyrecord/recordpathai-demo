Protect your account with two-step verification

To keep client records safe, RecordPathAI requires a free authenticator app on your phone. This is a one-time setup. You will use a fresh six-digit code each time verification is required.

1. Open or install an authenticator app

On your phone, open Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator. If you don't have one, search for either app in your phone's App Store or Google Play Store.

Already have the app? You don't need to install another one.

2. Add RecordPathAI to the app

In your authenticator app, tap the plus (+) or Add account button. Choose Scan a QR code and point your phone at the code below.

Using the same phone? Enter the setup key instead

Choose Enter setup key or manual entry in your authenticator app. Name the account RecordPathAI Professional, select Time based (TOTP), and enter the complete setup key shown below.

This long key belongs in the authenticator app only. Do not enter it into the six-digit field on this website.

Once the app displays a six-digit number next to RecordPathAI Professional, you're ready for the next step.

Keep the QR code and setup key private. Never share them or your six-digit codes.

Enter your six-digit code

Open your authenticator app and find the entry named RecordPathAI Professional. Type the current six-digit number here, for example 123456 (example only). If it changes before you finish, use the new number.
