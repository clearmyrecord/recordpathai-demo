What happens when a record is sealed or expunged?

A refreshed report can omit cases that are no longer publicly available. The importer must compare a complete refreshed period against the same prior period and suppress missing records, rather than treating a monthly feed as a replacement.

This is a simulated suppression example, not confirmation that any real court case has been sealed. A snapshot alone cannot provide immediate notification of future changes.

How a court data feed would work

1. Receive

Accept an authorized court-generated fixed-width extract through a controlled, private process.

2. Normalize

Link each case with its parties, aliases and charges, while omitting sensitive personal fields.

3. Verify

Check source permissions, record restrictions, identity, and current public status.

4. Suppress & correct

Remove records after a verified sealing/expungement update and accept free correction requests.
