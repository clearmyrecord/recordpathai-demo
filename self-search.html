Search your court records

Find the appropriate official court search. If an approved Wood County index becomes active, verified matches may also appear here. A missing result never proves that no record exists. No login or payment is required.

Search text stays in your browser for official-portal referrals. Only if the Wood County approved index is enabled will submitting send your name or case number to RecordPathAI's separate search service by private POST. Court websites have their own privacy practices.

After you find a case

Verify the identity and disposition using the court's official docket before taking action. Then explore a free eligibility check or correction and removal options. RecordPathAI is independent and is not a court or law firm.

This is not a background-screening tool and must not be used for employment, rental, credit, or insurance eligibility decisions. Records may be confidential, sealed, expunged, corrected, or missing from the online portal.
