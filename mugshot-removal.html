Florida: Under Florida Statutes § 901.43, qualifying publishers must remove a booking photo without charge within 10 calendar days after receiving a compliant written request. The statute specifies registered mail to the publisher's registered agent, sufficient proof of identity, and identifying information for the photo. This generated letter alone is not proof of statutory delivery. Read the law.
Florida registered-mail checklist
For a statutory request to a qualifying publisher, verify its registered agent and mailing address, print the letter, include sufficient identification, and send it by registered mail. Keep your mailing receipt and delivery confirmation. The 10-calendar-day period begins when the publisher receives the compliant request, not when you generate this letter.
