Current End-to-End Workflow

RecordPathAI is live today with a guided process that helps users move from free screening to court filing preparation while keeping users in control of final review and filing.

1

Check Eligibility Free

Start with a no-cost eligibility check to see whether your case may qualify before paying for packet generation.

2

Enter Intake Information

Provide personal intake and case-state information. The selected case state controls which rules, court path, and packet logic apply throughout the workflow.

3

Add Record Details

Enter offense and case details in structured fields, including charge, statute or offense code, court, county, case number, relevant dates, disposition or outcome, and case state.

4

Review Eligibility on the Packet Page

Eligibility results are shown on packet.html after intake and record details are combined, not on record-details.html, so the decision reflects the full case context.

5

Generate Your Court Packet

When supported and eligible, RecordPathAI generates printable, mapped PDF filing packets designed for court submission workflows.

6

Complete Secure Payment

Secure online payment is live and active for packet generation and related workflow steps.

7

File With the Court

Download, print, review, sign, and file with the court directly by mail or in person as required. RecordPathAI does not currently e-file with courts.

What RecordPathAI Does Not Do Yet

  • RecordPathAI does not e-file directly with every court yet.
  • RecordPathAI does not replace an attorney.
  • RecordPathAI does not guarantee court approval.
  • RecordPathAI does not remove fingerprinting, hearings, filing fees, clerk review, notarization, or other local court requirements.

Important Court Responsibility Notice

Users are responsible for reviewing all information, signing documents, printing and mailing or filing in person, and following local court rules. Some courts may require fingerprints, extra forms, filing fees, hearings, notarization, or additional clerk steps before acceptance.
