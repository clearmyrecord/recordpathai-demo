How referrals will work

1. Apply & get approved

We review partner identity, audience, advertising approach and conflicts before activating any unique referral link.

2. Share transparently

Customers visit RecordPathAI through your tracked link and sign in. Attribution is first-touch per account with a 30-day purchase window; only the first eligible partner is credited.

3. Earn after verified payment

Our server verifies Stripe payment and attributes commission to approved partners, with a 30-day hold for reversals. Payouts require owner review, tax clearance, Stripe recipient verification and a $50 threshold.

Important: Affiliates cannot promise that someone qualifies, imply court or government endorsement, solicit or publish private case data, or charge prospective customers for referrals. Paid promotion must clearly disclose the relationship near the recommendation, for example: “I may earn a commission if you purchase through my link.” FTC disclosure guidance.

Restricted partner categories: No payments to judges, clerks, court employees, public officials acting in their official role, probation/parole officers, or people for referring individuals they supervise. Attorney/legal-service relationships require separate attorney-ethics review; no sharing of legal fees or paid legal-client recommendations. We reserve the right to reject a partner, withhold disputed/fraudulent commissions, and comply with tax and identity-verification requirements.
