Why people seek record relief

More opportunities. More privacy. A clearer path forward.

A past case can make everyday decisions harder. When you qualify, sealing or expungement may help reduce some of those barriers.

Employment opportunities

Limit the visibility of eligible records in many ordinary background checks when applying for work.

Housing opportunities

Reduce a possible obstacle when applying to rent a home or apartment.

Education and training

Pursue education, credentials and career training with fewer record-related barriers where applicable.

Professional and business goals

Move toward professional credentials or a business goal, subject to any disclosure and licensing rules.

Personal privacy

Limit general public access to qualifying court records; sealing does not necessarily destroy records or remove third-party copies.

Your next chapter

Take a practical step toward moving forward with greater confidence.

Benefits vary by jurisdiction and type of relief. Some government agencies, regulated employers and licensing bodies may retain access. Sealing does not guarantee jobs, housing, licenses or removal from every website.

Questions before you begin?

A few important things to know.

Does an arrest mean I was convicted?

No. The official court disposition is important for understanding what happened and what legal options may be available.

Does RecordPathAI search my personal record on this homepage?

No. The court finder identifies official court websites. Enter names and case numbers on the court's own portal or in the guided workflow.

Does the free eligibility intake include court filing fees?

The eligibility check is free and does not include court fees. The planned $50 packet service and any court fees will be presented separately when signed-packet preparation and fulfillment launch.

Can I order a signed packet and have it mailed now?

Not yet. We are finalizing court-specific signed PDF generation, payment handling and Lob print-and-mail fulfillment. You can complete the free eligibility process and save your case details in the meantime.

Is RecordWatch Premium billing live?

RecordWatch account tools and the dashboard are available. Monthly Premium billing has been tested with Stripe test payments. Real subscription charges are not enabled yet; planned pricing is $4.99 monthly or $39 annually.
