Questions People Ask Before They Start

Record clearing and sealing rules depend on your state, court, charges, case outcome, and waiting periods. RecordPathAI helps you gather the right information so you can better understand your options.

How do I know if I may qualify?

Start with the eligibility check. RecordPathAI reviews the information you provide and shows possible next steps.

Can RecordPathAI help if I am confused by court paperwork?

Yes. The service guides you through the information needed to prepare court-ready forms.

Does RecordPathAI file documents for me?

No. RecordPathAI helps you prepare paperwork. You review, sign, and submit it to the appropriate court.

Is RecordPathAI a law firm?

No. RecordPathAI is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.
