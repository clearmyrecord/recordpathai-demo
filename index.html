Why people seek record relief

More opportunities. More privacy. A clearer path forward.

A past case can make everyday decisions harder. When you qualify, sealing or expungement may help reduce some of those barriers.

Questions before you begin?

A few important things to know.

Does an arrest mean I was convicted?

No. The official court disposition is important for understanding what happened and what legal options may be available.

Does RecordPathAI search my personal record on this homepage?

No. The court finder identifies official court websites. Enter names and case numbers on the court's own portal or in the guided workflow.

Does the free eligibility intake include court filing fees?

No. The eligibility check is free. RecordWatch Premium is $39 once per saved case; the planned $50 packet service and applicable court fees are separate. The Wood County Common Pleas conviction example totals $189 when the full filing service becomes available. See the breakdown.

Can I start RecordWatch on the day of my arrest?

You can begin saving case details as early as your arrest. But filing eligibility depends on the final outcome, charge, statutory waiting periods and final discharge where required. RecordWatch does not promise that a case can be sealed immediately.

Can multiple cases share one payment?

Where a court allows multiple cases or charges on the same application, our intended fee is per application instead of charging duplicates. The current live $39 Stripe checkout is still per saved case. Contact us before purchasing separate upgrades for cases that may be filed together.

Can I order a signed packet and have it mailed now?

Not yet. We are finalizing court-specific signed PDF generation, payment handling and Lob print-and-mail fulfillment. You can complete the free eligibility process and save your case details in the meantime.

Is RecordWatch Premium billing live?

Yes. The $39 one-time Stripe Checkout is live for one saved case, with lifetime Premium access and no automatic renewal. We verified payment activation, webhook delivery and refund revocation. Email and SMS are Premium-only; actual deliveries depend on the connected messaging providers. Multiple cases on the same court application should be reviewed before buying duplicate case upgrades.
