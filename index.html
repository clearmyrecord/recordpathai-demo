COMMON QUESTIONS

Know before you act.

Does an arrest record mean someone was convicted?

No. An arrest and a conviction are different. Verify the court disposition before drawing conclusions.

Does RecordPathAI guarantee removal?

No. Eligibility and results vary by jurisdiction, record type, and third-party policies. We do not promise a specific result.

Can I correct a listing on this site without paying?

Yes. Corrections and requests to remove information published by RecordPathAI are free and do not require buying a service.

Are search results live?

The homepage finds official portals in Ohio, Nevada, and Florida; it does not fetch or verify individual cases. The separate guided Wood County pilot is restricted until authorized, current-status-verified court data is available. An empty response does not prove that no record exists.

Is RecordPathAI part of the court system?

No. RecordPathAI is an independent informational service, not a court, law firm, or government agency. Court orders and published filing instructions control.

Can the software file my paperwork for me?

Do not assume that direct e-filing is available. Packet tools and payment options vary by jurisdiction and workflow. Review every form, court requirement, filing fee, and submission method before taking action.
