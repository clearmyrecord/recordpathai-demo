What RecordWatch Does

RecordWatch helps users reuse RecordPathAI intake data, organize charges and outcomes, estimate sealing or expungement eligibility, track missing requirements, prepare next-step action plans, and monitor whether records have been corrected after relief. Advanced Case Intake is optional for arrest, booking, prosecutor, judge, defense attorney, jail, and detailed monitoring information.

Collect early

Capture arrest, booking, court, charge, and completion details in one local workflow.

Estimate next steps

Use rules-based estimates to identify possible eligibility dates, missing requirements, and review flags.

Monitor corrections

Track court dockets, repositories, background-check companies, data brokers, and verified updates.

Four-Layer Workflow

Layer 1

Unified Intake Bridge

Personal, case, charge, outcome, and completion data are captured from the existing RecordPathAI workflow first.

Layer 2

Rules & Eligibility Engine

Estimated waiting periods, missing requirements, risk flags, and recommended status.

Layer 3

Monitoring Engine

Saved checklists for court, repository, background-check, data-broker, and follow-up tasks.

Layer 4

Automated Relief Engine

Action plans, packet recommendations, packet prefill, and dispute/correction letters.

Why This Matters

Court systems, state repositories, background-check companies, data brokers, and employers are fragmented. A person may be eligible for relief years before they know it, and even after relief is granted, private databases may still show outdated information.
