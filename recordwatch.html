What RecordWatch Does

RecordWatch is a localStorage-based case tracking workspace for organizing relief information before, during, and after a court filing.

Collect and organize

  • Collect case data early
  • Organize charges and outcomes
  • Track missing court and completion information

Estimate and monitor

  • Estimate sealing/expungement eligibility
  • Prepare next-step action plans
  • Monitor whether records have been corrected after relief

Four-Layer Workflow

Layer 1

Record Intake Engine

Capture personal, arrest, booking, court, charge, disposition, and completion details in a structured case file.

Layer 2

Rules & Eligibility Engine

Use placeholder state-rule logic to estimate status, waiting periods, missing requirements, and risk flags.

Layer 3

Monitoring Engine

Track court, repository, background-check, data-broker, dispute, follow-up, and correction verification steps.

Layer 4

Automated Relief Engine

Build action plans, prefill court-packet data, and generate user-prepared dispute/correction requests.

Why This Matters

Court systems, state repositories, background-check companies, data brokers, and employers are fragmented. A person may be eligible for relief years before they know it, and even after relief is granted, private databases may still show outdated information.
