# Professional live billing operator checklist
- Production prices are configured for $99/mo solo, $249/mo firm and $10 optional filing; existing consumer price IDs and checkout paths are unchanged. Stripe Tax uses account defaults and checkout displays applicable tax separately.
- Verify applicant's Ohio bar registration, authority to bind their organization, signed partner terms, security/legal review, and verified email. Create a dedicated LIVE Stripe customer (no payment method or charge required), metadata product_family=recordpathai_professional and authorized_user=<verified auth user UUID>. Never reuse a consumer customer.
- Database owner calls professional_approve_paid_request with the verified user UUID, dedicated live customer ID, and actual documented reference IDs. Do not enter placeholder evidence. This preapproval does not grant client intake; account page exposes the approved plan checkout.
- Checkout requires explicit recurring-payment consent. On return, a direct server-side Stripe check validates account, live price, exact subscription and captured latest invoice payment, excluding disputed or refunded charges. Access lease is at most 20 minutes and reverified on workspace entry and every ten minutes. No browser write to entitlement. Payment issues revoke access on next successful verification; unreachable verification expires the lease. No payment is taken for document preparation or manual filing beyond the subscription.
- Dedicated portal permits invoices, payment method changes, and end-of-period cancellation; no plan switching. Operator must review any plan or firm membership changes. The portal configuration became Stripe's default because no prior configuration existed; professional sessions explicitly select it.
- Before onboarding actual paying customers, verify full sandbox checkout and approved-account browser workflow; confirm tax registrations, client export/retention and access upon nonpayment, partner terms, support and cancellation procedures. Currently no active production organizations/applications. No real card or court submission was used for tests.
- Automated filing remains disabled, including collection of $10 and court deposit, until exact signed-document approval, professional Lob fulfillment, court acceptance, actual cost and idempotency tests are complete. Attorney-only signing and live court-source imports are unverified. Manual filing instructions and court costs remain separate.
